# Attribution

`karpathy-guidelines` ports all four principles of the Karpathy guidelines.

- Upstream: multica-ai/andrej-karpathy-skills
  (https://github.com/multica-ai/andrej-karpathy-skills)
- License: MIT
- Source material: `skills/karpathy-guidelines/SKILL.md` and `README.md`
  (reviewed 2026-10-04)

Principle 2 ("Simplicity First") overlaps the `ponytail` skill; the SKILL.md
says so and defers to `ponytail` as the authority when both are loaded.

Original additions in this port: the ask-vs-proceed threshold in principle 1,
the closing checklist (assumptions, diff review, orphans, evidenced success
criteria), the "how to know it's working" signals, and the precedence rule
deferring to project conventions.
